Three visual directions

All three show the same Home screen: Alex, day 23 of a recomp, mid-afternoon. Only the look changes. The layout follows PRD §6.7: calories left, protein/carbs/fat, the phase card, and one insight, with no more than four cards. Every number is sample data.

AInstrument

Dark, precise and quiet, like a fitness wearable's app. Numbers in a monospace face, one cool teal accent.

How to choose

Reply with A, B or C. Mixing works too, for example "A's layout with C's colors" or "B, but dark". The pick becomes the prototype's look, and every other screen gets built in it.
