Leanward · Prototype · Visual identity, round 2

Five directions, each built on one idea

Same screen every time: Alex, day 23 of a recomp, mid-afternoon, 920 kcal left. Same content and order (PRD §6.7). What changes is the idea each look is built on. All numbers are sample data.

  • Numbers are the heroPeople glance at this app between sets and in the kitchen. The calories-left figure has to read in under a second.
  • Calm, never alarmingNo red "over" states. Status uses shape and words as well as color (PRD §9, LOG-13).
  • One accent, used rarelyThe accent marks the one thing to act on (the + button, the main number), so it keeps its meaning.
  • Owns its ideaEach look has one signature element no other tracker has.

01Contour

Progress as terrain. A phase is a route; you are the marker on it.

Pale alpine light, topographic lines and a trail-marker orange used only for "you are here" and the + button. Wide, sturdy type, like map labels and outdoor gear.

02Forward

The name, made visual: everything leans forward.

Athletic and confident. Condensed italic numerals, slanted bars and a solid cobalt block for the day's number, like a race bib. It uses Barlow, the same family as PR Forge, so the two apps feel related.

03Plate

Color comes from food. Each macro has the color of what it's made of.

Protein is salmon, carbs are grain gold, fat is olive. The calorie ring is literally your plate so far, split by macro. Bright, appetizing and friendly, with a warm serif for headings and numbers.

04Phase Light

The phase sets the color of the whole app.

Deep night with a soft glow of light at the top. The glow's color tells you which phase you're in: cool for a cut, iris for a recomp, ember for a bulk. Starting a new phase changes the light in the app.

05Marker

A coach's whiteboard. Huge numbers, one highlighter.

Black on warm grey, heavy grotesque numerals, no cards, only thick rules. A single highlighter yellow marks the one number that matters right now. It's the fastest to read at a glance.

My recommendation

04 Phase Light is the strongest brand idea, because it makes Leanward's core concept visible: you can see which phase you're in. It's also closest to the calm, premium feel the PRD describes. 02 Forward is the best choice if you want Leanward and PR Forge to look like one family.

They combine well: Phase Light's tinted glow with Forward's leaning italic numbers. Reply with a number, a mix (for example "4 with 2's numbers"), or "none of these" and what's missing.
