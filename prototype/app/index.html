Leanward · Prototype v0.2

Try the main flows

A clickable mock of the MVP in Phase Light. Everything works on sample data; nothing is saved to a server.

  • Onboarding: units, about you, optional body fat and waist, activity, goal, phase, targets with the working, food preferences
  • + button: recent, search, simulated barcode scan, quick add, saved meals, custom foods, plus weigh-ins and measurements
  • Diary: edit, move or delete entries; ⋯ on a meal to save it or copy it to another day; Nutrients shows data coverage
  • Progress: log weight, waist and other sites, body fat and lifts; charts; All entries to edit or delete
  • Phase: progress read, edit targets (past days keep theirs), target history, end-of-phase prompt, start a new phase
  • Profile: appearance, units, diary slots, key lifts, food preferences, my foods, reminders, export and delete

Sample person: Alex, 31, recomp day 23. "Today" is fixed to Wed, Nov 25, 2026. Food values are approximate USDA figures for demonstration.
