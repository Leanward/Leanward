Privacy

Leanward, version in development · last updated Nov 3, 2026

What this early version stores

Your account. You sign in with Google. Google tells Leanward your name, email address, profile picture link and an account ID, and Leanward's database (Supabase) keeps them so you can sign in again. Leanward never sees your Google password and gets no access to your Gmail, contacts or files.

There's no diary or measurements in this build yet, so nothing else about you is stored.

Error reports. If the app hits a bug, it sends a short technical report to Leanward's own database: a masked error message (quoted text and numbers removed), which file and line failed, the app version and your browser's name and version. It never includes food, weight, measurements or anything you typed. No other company receives these reports.

On your phone. A copy of the app's files is kept so it opens offline, plus your sign-in (so you stay signed in), your name and email, and your last-opened tab. Signing out removes the sign-in from the phone.

What's planned

When the diary arrives, your data will be stored in your own rows in Leanward's database, which only you can read. There will be no ads, and health or nutrition data will never be sold or shared. This page will be updated before any of that ships.
