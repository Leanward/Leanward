Privacy

Leanward, version in development · last updated Nov 2, 2026

What this early version stores

Nothing about you yet. There's no sign-in, diary or measurements in this build.

Error reports. If the app hits a bug, it sends a short technical report to Leanward's own database: a masked error message (quoted text and numbers removed), which file and line failed, the app version and your browser's name and version. It never includes food, weight, measurements or anything you typed. No other company receives these reports.

On your phone. A copy of the app's files is kept so it opens offline, plus your last-opened tab.

What's planned

When sign-in and the diary arrive, your data will be stored in your own rows in Leanward's database, which only you can read. There will be no ads, and health or nutrition data will never be sold or shared. This page will be updated before any of that ships.
